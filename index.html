Our Work

Crane Course

Our first app is a golf companion for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android. It shows front, center and back yardages to the green from your device's own Global Positioning System, and keeps working offline once a course is downloaded.

  • Phone and watch togetherThe watch uses its own location, so the phone can stay in the cart.
  • Honest numbersReports location accuracy plainly and hides yardages when you're away from the course.
  • No account, advertising or trackingScores and rounds stay on your phone and watch.
Built Into Crane Course

Off Course, a game while you wait.

A 3D golf game of Lakeside Country Club in Penn Yan, New York. All 18 holes are built from the real course's shapes, aerial imagery and laser elevation surveys. It comes free with the app, needs no extra download and plays offline.
